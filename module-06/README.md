# Module 6 — Problem Definition and Information Gathering

**Course:** EN.585.617 Rehabilitation Engineering (FA26). Two assignments, both due Day 7.

## 1. Research paper
`Module_06_Research_Paper_Pressure_Injuries.(docx|pdf)` — 4 pages: ~2.5 pages of body
(Introduction, Detection, Prevention, Treatment, Discussion/Conclusion) plus references,
matching the "2-3 page, plus references" spec. **10 published sources**, IEEE citation style.

**Position taken:** the aetiology of pressure injury has been re-established on mechanical
rather than vascular foundations (deformation primary, inflammation secondary, ischaemia
tertiary), but detection still keys on a visible skin change that occurs *after* irreversible
subcutaneous damage — and that lag propagates into staging, risk scoring and trial endpoints,
which is why the prevention evidence is so uniformly low-certainty.
