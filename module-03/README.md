## Argument
BSM was validated on a general driving population that retains the shoulder check as a backup.
For a driver who cannot turn their head it is the sole means of clearing the lane, which
converts three known weaknesses into primary hazards — a mirror-mounted icon that still demands
a head movement, an audible escalation on a crowded channel, and the ambiguity of silence
(a clear lane and a dead sensor produce the identical non-signal).

## Modifications
1. **Directional seat-bolster haptic alerting** — LRAs in the left and right seat-back bolsters
   driven off the existing BSM CAN message. The seat is a seat-fixed reference frame; a
   steering-wheel actuator rotates with the wheel and loses its left/right mapping exactly
   during the merge when the alert matters most.
2. **Positive system-health confirmation on the same channel** — ignition self-test pulse plus a
   distinct bilateral triple-burst when radar return statistics indicate an occluded aperture.
   Resolves the ambiguity of silence that Modification 1 would otherwise deepen.
